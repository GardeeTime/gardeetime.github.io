## Why this followed Account Research

Once reps trusted account-level context, the obvious next ask surfaced: *who am I actually talking to, what do they care about, and what have we already said to each other?* The agent answers that.

## What I drove

- **Source mix.** CRM (relationship history, prior touches), web (titles, role tenure, content signals), engagement data (open/click/reply patterns), and — the unlock — call transcripts. Past-conversation continuity is the thing reps undervalue and customers notice.
- **Surface choice.** Embedded the briefing in the rep's existing prep flow rather than building a new dashboard. The point was to not introduce another tool to check.
- **Quality bar.** Same prompt + eval discipline as Account Research. Ran the agent on real prospects, scored outputs against rep notes, refined.

## Outcome

Shipped. Adopted into the rep prep loop. Closed the loop on the "who am I calling" half of pre-call prep that Account Research had left open.
