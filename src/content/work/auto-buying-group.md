## The hidden gap

When I dug into the data, more than 9 in 10 active opportunities didn't have all stakeholders identified in the CRM. Reps weren't lazy — manual buying-group capture was just brutal busy-work nobody had time for.

## What I drove

- **Signal mining.** Engagement activity (emails opened/replied, meetings attended, mentions in calls) is rich enough to infer who's involved in a deal. The trick is the inference logic.
- **Accuracy bar.** Set a target of 95%+ stakeholder-identification accuracy before shipping. Underestimated stakeholders erode trust faster than no recommendation; better to surface fewer and be right.
- **Surface.** Recommendations in the deal view, with one-click confirm/reject. Made it almost zero-effort for reps to keep the buying group accurate.

## Outcome

Shipped to GA. Closed the buying-group data gap that had quietly tanked forecast accuracy across the platform.
