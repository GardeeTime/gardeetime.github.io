## The friction

Reps building a prospect list had to bounce between the engagement platform, the CRM, and a third-party data source. Each switch leaked time and broke flow. The agent collapsed all three.

## What I drove

- **Partner integration.** OAuth-based connection with a major contact-data provider. Negotiated the scope, designed the consent flow, drove security review.
- **Surface design.** A single in-product picker that returned both existing CRM contacts (with relationship state) and net-new ICP-fit prospects (with enrichment metadata) in one view.
- **Match logic.** ICP definitions came from the customer's existing setup — not a new schema. The agent inherited their criteria instead of forcing them to redefine.

## Outcome

Shipped. Removed a major source of context-switching from the rep prospecting flow. Foundational for the broader buying-group capture work that came next.
