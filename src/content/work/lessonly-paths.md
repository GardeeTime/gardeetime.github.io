## The friction

Customers had hundreds of lessons but no structured way to deliver them. Onboarding a new hire meant a manager hand-assigning lessons one by one, in the right order, with the right pacing. Repetitive busy-work that broke whenever a lesson got renamed.

## What I shipped

- **Sequenced content groups.** Admins could bundle lessons into a Path with ordering, prerequisites, and completion logic. Once defined, the Path ran itself for every new learner.
- **Certification surface.** Once a learner completed the Path, a formal certification artifact got generated — useful for compliance, customer-facing trust signals, and internal career progression.
- **Drift protection.** Paths stayed valid when component lessons were edited or replaced — admins didn't have to rebuild every time content moved.

## Outcome

Shipped. Removed the single biggest source of admin manual work in onboarding.
