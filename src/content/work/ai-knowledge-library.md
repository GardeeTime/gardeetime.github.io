## What it is

A knowledge surface reps reach for during calls and email follow-ups — product docs, sales-objection responses, competitive battlecards — retrieved with conversational queries instead of folder-tree spelunking.

## What I drove

- **Discovery.** Surveyed where reps actually got their knowledge today (Slack searches, sales-leader DMs, reading old call recordings) and the friction of each path.
- **Solution architecture.** Defined the content model, the retrieval surface, and the writeback loop (rep flags content as helpful or stale → librarian sees it → content gets curated). Knowledge bases die when nobody owns them; the writeback loop keeps it alive.
- **UX.** Embedded conversational query in the existing rep view. Result: fewer tabs, more actual reading.

## Outcome

Shipped. Adopted into the rep workflow.
