## How I found it

I watched a sales-enablement leader run an in-person role-play session. A rep would pitch the product to a coach, get feedback, redo it. The format worked — but it required everyone in the same room at the same time, and most teams couldn't sustain that cadence.

## What I shipped

- **The tech bet.** Browser-based screen + webcam recording without installs. The friction of "install software on every rep's machine" had killed previous attempts. Browser-native unlocked it.
- **The format.** A rep records a pitch attempt against a scenario; a coach reviews and leaves time-coded feedback. Async. Repeatable. Reviewable.
- **The viral surface.** Coaches sharing recordings with new hires, peers, and leadership turned Practice into a content asset, not a tool. The recording library became a reason to log in.

## Outcome

Shipped. Adopted by 500+ customer teams. Became one of Lessonly's defining product surfaces.
