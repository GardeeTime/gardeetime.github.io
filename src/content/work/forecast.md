## How I found it

Spent a week shadowing sales leaders. Watched what they actually did when their VP asked "where will we land this quarter?" Almost universally: a Google Sheet copy-paste from the CRM, a lot of margin notes, and a gut adjustment. People managing nine-figure pipelines were running it on copy-pasted rows.

## What I shipped

- **30-day v1.** Built the smallest thing that would replace the spreadsheet for one specific question — *will we hit the number?* Stripped every adjacency. Shipped and learned.
- **Iteration on real customers.** First-year roadmap was driven by what early adopters did *next* with the v1, not by what the spec said v2 should be.
- **Cross-functional pull.** Worked across data, design, and revenue ops to make the forecast feel like a single product, not three integrations stitched together.

## Outcome

150+ customers in year one. Millions in net-new ARR. The product became one of the platform's anchor revenue surfaces.
